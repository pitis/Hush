# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person, the owner, using Hush every day for Signal on a Mac running macOS 26, alongside
their phone. Most sessions are short: glance, reply, back to work. Some are long conversations
or calls.

## Product Purpose

A native Mac client for Signal: read and reply fast, keep history, make calls, without the
Electron app. Success: it feels like it belongs on the Mac and nothing about it gets in the way
of the conversation.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, native. The conversation is the loudest thing on screen; chrome recedes. Every
alignment and type step is deliberate. Where macOS has a component or behaviour, Hush uses it.

References: Messages.app (native structure, Liquid Glass, little chrome of its own), Telegram
for macOS (polished density and bubbles), Linear and Raycast (precision, restraint, keyboard
first).

## Anti-references

- A web app in a window: flat opaque panels, custom title bars, web spacing (Signal Desktop).
- Glass everywhere: blur and translucency as decoration instead of structure.
- Busy or colourful: accent colour, badges and icons competing for attention.
- A generic chat template or AI-made mockup.

## Design Principles

1. System first: native window, sidebar, toolbar and controls; custom only where macOS has nothing.
2. Content over chrome: messages carry the colour and weight; controls stay quiet until needed.
3. Glass for structure, not decoration: it marks floating layers (toolbar, composer), never content.
4. Precise and consistent: one type scale, one spacing rhythm, aligned edges.
5. Say less: notices are rare, use names rather than IDs, and collapse when they repeat.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follows system settings: Reduce Transparency (glass falls back to solid), Reduce Motion,
Increase Contrast, light and dark appearance. Every control has a VoiceOver label. Text meets
WCAG AA contrast.
